# Privacy Policy
Last updated: July 17, 2026

LoveHub ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

## Information We Collect

**Account info:** Email and display name you provide at sign-up (or via Google Sign-In), stored in Firebase Authentication.

**Couple data:** Relationship start date, pairing code, daily check-ins, streak counters, moods, memories (including photos), saving jar contributions, and virtual pet progress.

**Photos & media:** Images you upload are sent to Cloudinary (our image hosting provider) and stored as signed URLs in Firebase Firestore. Only your linked partner can view them.

**Push notification IDs:** OneSignal assigns a device token used to route notifications. It is stored in Firestore under your user document.

**App usage:** We do NOT collect browsing history, location, contacts, SMS, microphone, or camera data. LoveHub does not use analytics trackers beyond Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports only) and Firebase Performance Monitoring (anonymous app-speed metrics).

## How We Use Your Information

- Provide core features: couple pairing, day counter, check-ins, mood tracking, memories, saving jars, virtual pet, and AI chat assistant.
- Sync couple data between you and your partner in real time via Firebase Firestore.
- Send you reminders and partner-activity notifications you have enabled.
- Detect and diagnose app crashes (Crashlytics) and measure app speed (Performance Monitoring).

## Data Sharing

- Your couple data is accessible only to you and your linked partner. It is protected by Firebase Security Rules.
- We do NOT sell your personal data to advertisers or third parties.
- Third-party service providers (Firebase, Cloudinary, OneSignal, Groq AI) process your data only to deliver their services. Each provider's privacy policy applies.

## Data Storage & Retention

- Data is stored on Firebase (Google Cloud) in the United States.
- You can delete your account at any time via Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes all your personal data from Firebase.
- If you unpair from your partner, your couple-scoped data (memories, streaks, pet, etc.) remains with your partner's account.

## Security

We use Firebase Authentication (secure token-based auth), Firestore Security Rules, TLS encryption for all network traffic, certificate pinning in the app, and Google Play Integrity API checks to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## Children's Privacy

LoveHub is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are in the EU or California, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your data. Contact us at the email below. We will respond within 30 days.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will notify you of material changes via an in-app notice. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.
